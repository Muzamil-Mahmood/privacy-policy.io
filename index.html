1. Introduction

Welcome to GymLedger ("the App", "we", "us", "our"), a gym member management application for gym owners, fitness studios and their staff. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you download, register for and use GymLedger, how that information is used, with whom it is shared, and the choices and rights available to you.

By creating an account or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

In short: You are a gym owner or staff member. You enter information about your gym and your members into GymLedger. We provide the software to store and process that information securely. We do not sell your data or your members' data.

2. Data We Collect

We collect information in three ways: information you provide directly, information generated as you use the App, and information collected by third-party services embedded in the App.

2.1 Account & authentication data

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Password (stored only in encrypted form by our authentication provider — we never see or store your password in plain text)
  • Email verification status and account status (active / disabled)
  • Staff role and permissions (owner or staff member)

2.2 Gym profile data

  • Gym or studio name, logo, address
  • Contact phone number and contact email of the gym
  • Country, time zone and operating currency

2.3 Member, subscription & payment data you enter

Important: The App is used by gyms to manage their own members. When you add a member to GymLedger, you confirm that you have a lawful basis to enter that person's personal information into the App (for example, the member is your customer).

  • Member full name, member ID, phone number (with country code), email address, joining date and optional notes
  • Membership plans, prices, subscription start and end dates, renewal history and status
  • Payment records: amount, date, method (cash, bank transfer or other) and optional reference
  • Staff invitation records and audit history (who changed what and when)

2.4 Device & usage data

  • Local device storage (preferences such as last used settings) stored on your device
  • Log and diagnostic information processed by our cloud providers for security and reliability

2.5 Advertising data

  • Google Advertising ID and related advertising identifiers (see Section 5)

2.6 Data we do NOT collect

GymLedger does not collect: health records, medical information, workout or nutrition data, biometric data, identity documents, location of members, payment card numbers, or bank credentials. The App records that a payment happened; it does not process money.

3. How We Use Data

We use collected information to:

  • Create, maintain and secure your account (registration, login, email verification, password recovery)
  • Provide the core features of the App: member management, membership plans, subscriptions and renewals, payment recording, balances, dashboards, reports and audit history
  • Calculate membership statuses (active, expiring soon, expired) and show expiry alerts
  • Enable staff invitations and enforce owner/staff permissions
  • Import and export member data as directed by you (CSV import / export)
  • Display advertisements (free version), measure ad performance and prevent fraud
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot errors
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests

We do not use the content of your member records to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or rent your personal data to anyone.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

GymLedger displays advertisements served by Google AdMob (a Google LLC service) in the free version of the App. AdMob may collect and process information including:

  • Your Google Advertising ID (AAID) and device information (model, operating system, language, carrier)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address (country/region level)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)
  • Data from cookies or similar technologies used for ad measurement and frequency capping

Your advertising choices: You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization). You can also manage ad personalization at adssettings.google.com and review Google's policies at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Where required by law (for example the EU ePrivacy Directive and GDPR), personalized advertising is only activated after consent. GymLedger uses Google's UMP (User Messaging Platform) consent flow when applicable to your region.

7. Retention & Deletion

  • Account data is retained while your account is active.
  • Gym records (members, subscriptions, payments) are retained until you delete them or close your account.
  • Archived members remain stored (with history) until permanently deleted.
  • After account closure, remaining copies in active systems are removed within a reasonable period, and backups are aged out on the standard backup retention schedule of our cloud provider.
  • Advertiser data is retained by Google under Google's own retention rules.

Account or data deletion: You can delete your account or request export/deletion of your data at any time by contacting us (Section 13). Owners may export member, subscription and payment data as CSV before deletion.

8. Security

  • Data in transit is encrypted using TLS (HTTPS)
  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text
  • Cloud data is encrypted at rest by our hosting provider
  • Server-side authorization: every request verifies your identity, role and gym membership — no access unless explicitly allowed
  • Gym data isolation: records are scoped per gym; altering identifiers never exposes another gym's data
  • Role permissions: staff cannot change billing settings, ownership or export the entire database
  • Audit history logs who performed important actions

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Please use a strong password and keep it confidential. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately.

9. Children's Privacy

GymLedger is a business tool intended for gym owners and staff aged 16 years or older (13 where local law permits a higher age of digital consent, parents may consent). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age).

Gym member records entered by you may in rare cases include minors; you are responsible for having a lawful basis to record that data. If you believe a child's personal data has been entered into the App without proper authorisation, contact us and we will work with the gym owner to remove it.

10. International Transfers

GymLedger uses Google Cloud (Firebase) infrastructure. Data may be stored and processed in the United States and in other countries where Google or its sub-processors operate, which may have different data protection laws than your country.

For users in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, transfers are protected by Google's Standard Contractual Clauses and related safeguards as required by applicable law. Google Cloud's compliance certifications are available at cloud.google.com/security/compliance.

11. Your Rights

Subject to your local law, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you (as an account user)
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Delete your data (right to erasure)
  • Port your data in a structured, machine-readable format (CSV export)
  • Restrict or object to certain processing, including direct marketing
  • Withdraw consent at any time (for example ad personalization)
  • Complain to your local data protection supervisory authority

For gym members: If you are a member of a gym that uses GymLedger, please contact your gym directly first — they control your member record. We will assist the gym owner in responding to verified requests.
