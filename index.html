1. Overview

GymLedger (“the App”) is a gym management application that helps gym owners and staff manage members, membership plans, payments and reports. The App is operated by the gym or fitness business that uses it (the “Gym”), not by us as a data controller for end-member records.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the App, how it is used, stored and protected, and what choices you have. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

Plain summary: we collect only what a gym needs to run its membership administration. We do not sell personal data, we do not process card payments, and we do not use analytics or crash-tracking SDKs.

2. Information we collect

Account information

  • Name and email address used to register and sign in.
  • Password — handled entirely by Firebase Authentication (Google's industry-standard hashed credential storage). The App never stores your password.
  • Email verification status and password-reset flow metadata.
  • Your role at the Gym (owner or staff) and staff invitation details (name, email, role).

Gym & member records

Information entered by the Gym's staff through the App, which may include:

  • Gym profile: gym name, country, time zone, currency, optional address, contact phone and email.
  • Member records: full name, optional phone number, optional email, optional notes, join date, member number and reminder opt-in preference.
  • Membership plans and subscription periods.
We do not collect: member photos, government ID documents, health or medical information, biometric data, precise location, or contacts.

Payment records

  • Amount, currency, date, payment method category (cash, bank transfer, or other) and optional reference text.
  • GymLedger only records payments made offline — it never asks for or processes card numbers, bank credentials or any payment instrument details.

Usage & audit data

  • Internal audit events (who created, edited, exported or reversed a record, and when).
  • Standard technical data processed by advertising SDKs (see §5).

3. How we use information

We process information for the following purposes:

  • Provide, operate and maintain the App (sign-in, data sync, core features).
  • Let Gyms manage members, plans, subscriptions and payment records.
  • Send essential transactional emails: email verification and password resets.
  • Maintain audit logs so Gyms can track changes to their records.
  • Show advertisements (banner, app-open and rewarded ads) to support the free tier of the App.
  • Prevent abuse and keep the service secure.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling.

4. Where data is stored

App data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), secured per-user: each account's data is isolated under its own user profile, and our security rules deny access to anyone else's data.

Data may be processed on Google's servers in the United States or other countries where Google operates. Google is compliant with the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and similar mechanisms. Google's privacy practices are governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising (AdMob)

The App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID or IDFA), IP address, ad interactions and coarse location derived from IP address in order to serve and measure ads.

  • Ad types shown: banner, app-open and rewarded ads (a rewarded ad may be optional to unlock certain actions).
  • Whether ads are personalized depends on your consent and region. Where required (e.g., EEA/UK), Google's consent platform will ask you before personalized ads are used.
  • You can reset or limit your advertising ID and opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

AdMob's handling of data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

We do not sell or rent personal information. Data is shared only with:

7. Retention & deletion

  • Data is retained while your account is active, so the Gym can operate its records.
  • Deleting a member record removes it from the Gym's active list (archives are kept briefly for audit integrity, per the Gym's configuration).
  • You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting the Gym (for member records) or us (for account data) — see §12.
  • Backups may persist for a limited additional period before being permanently erased.

8. Security

  • All data in transit is encrypted with TLS.
  • Cloud Firestore data is encrypted at rest by Google.
  • Per-user security rules isolate each account's data.
  • Passwords are hashed and stored by Firebase Authentication, never by the App.
  • Owner-only CSV exports are audit-logged; exports never leave the device automatically.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we follow industry-standard practices to protect your information.

9. Your rights & choices

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g., GDPR, CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data (most records can be edited directly in the App by you or your Gym).
  • Delete your data (“right to be forgotten”).
  • Export your data in a portable format (owner accounts can export member CSVs from Settings).
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Opt out of personalized advertising via device settings (see §5).
  • Withdraw consent where processing is consent-based.

To exercise these rights, contact us at the address in §12. If you are a gym member whose record is managed by a Gym, please also contact that Gym directly — they control your member record.

EU/UK users: the responsible data controller for member records is the Gym using the App. Google acts as a processor for Firebase data (see Google's Data Processing Terms).

10. Children's privacy

The App is intended for use by gym businesses and their adult staff and members. It is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “effective date” at the top will change when we do. Material changes may also be announced in the App. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
